ActaRoot, Inc.

ActaRoot is a U.S. corporation founded by Min Sihu. It is the company behind OHWUT.

The official corporate website describes the company's identity and relationship to OHWUT.

OHWUT

OHWUT is the shopping brand associated with ActaRoot. Its platform combines ordinary online shopping with offers that become less expensive at higher participation levels.

Its official consumer destination is ohwut.com, where products and shopping information belong.

Founder's involvement

Min Sihu founded both ActaRoot and OHWUT. He identifies himself as the founder and CEO of OHWUT.

The founder, company and brand are distinct identities, linked by the relationship between them.
