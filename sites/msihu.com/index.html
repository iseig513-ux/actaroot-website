Official founder profile

Min Sihu

Min Sihu (Sihu Min; 민시후) is a 13-year-old founder based in South Korea. He founded ActaRoot, Inc. and the OHWUT shopping brand.

Work and projects

ActaRoot, Inc. is the U.S. corporation behind OHWUT. OHWUT serves U.S. shoppers, including products whose prices decrease as more people participate.
