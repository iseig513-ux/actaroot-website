Min Sihu

Founder of ActaRoot and OHWUT.

Founder profile

Min Sihu, also known as Sihu Min and 민시후, is a South Korea-based founder of ActaRoot, Inc. and OHWUT.

He connects a U.S. corporation with a shopping platform built around everyday online purchasing and participation-linked price reductions.

About Min Sihu

ActaRoot, Inc.

ActaRoot is the U.S. corporation founded by Min Sihu and the company behind OHWUT.

The company provides the corporate identity for the OHWUT brand, with official company information at actaroot.com.

ActaRoot

OHWUT

OHWUT is an online shopping platform serving the U.S. market. It combines ordinary shopping with offers where increased participation can move a product to a lower price level.

Min Sihu founded OHWUT and identifies himself as its founder and CEO.
