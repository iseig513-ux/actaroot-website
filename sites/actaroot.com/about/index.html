About ActaRoot

ActaRoot, Inc. is the company behind OHWUT. Min Sihu founded ActaRoot and OHWUT.

Company and founder

ActaRoot is a U.S. corporation. Its founder, Min Sihu, is based in South Korea.

ActaRoot and the OHWUT shopping brand are connected through the company's operation of the brand.
