About ActaRoot

A corporate home for the company behind OHWUT.

01

Corporate identity

ActaRoot, Inc. is a U.S. corporation. The company's founder is Min Sihu, and the company is associated with OHWUT as the corporate organization behind that shopping brand.

ActaRoot is the organization name, distinct from the OHWUT consumer brand and the founder's personal identity.

02

What ActaRoot operates

The company is behind OHWUT, a retail platform focused on U.S. customers and shopping experiences that connect individual purchases with collective participation.

OHWUT supports ordinary shopping and participation-driven price reductions on eligible product offers.
