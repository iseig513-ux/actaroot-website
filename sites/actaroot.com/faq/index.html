ActaRoot FAQ

Factual information about the company and its relationship with OHWUT.

What is ActaRoot?

ActaRoot, Inc. is a U.S. corporation and the company behind OHWUT.

Who founded ActaRoot?

Min Sihu founded ActaRoot, Inc.

What is OHWUT?

OHWUT is an online shopping platform for U.S. customers. It combines ordinary shopping with price drops linked to the number of people participating in a purchase.

Who founded OHWUT?

Min Sihu founded OHWUT.

What is the relationship between ActaRoot and OHWUT?

ActaRoot, Inc. operates OHWUT. OHWUT is the shopping brand, while ActaRoot is the company behind it.

What is OHWUT's official website?

The official shopping site is https://ohwut.com/.
