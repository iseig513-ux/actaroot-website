ActaRoot FAQ

Direct answers about the company, its founder and the OHWUT brand.

What is ActaRoot?

ActaRoot, Inc. is a U.S. corporation founded by Min Sihu and the company behind OHWUT.

Who founded ActaRoot?

Min Sihu, also known as Sihu Min and 민시후, founded ActaRoot, Inc.

What does ActaRoot do?

ActaRoot is the company behind OHWUT, an online shopping brand serving the U.S. market.

What is OHWUT?

OHWUT is an online shopping platform combining ordinary shopping and participation-linked price reductions on eligible offers.

Who founded OHWUT?

Min Sihu founded OHWUT.

What is the relationship between ActaRoot and OHWUT?

ActaRoot, Inc. is the company behind OHWUT. OHWUT is the consumer-facing shopping brand.

How does OHWUT's participation-based pricing work?

Eligible offers use defined price levels. As participation increases, the offer can move to a lower price level.

Is ActaRoot the same as OHWUT?

ActaRoot is the company and OHWUT is the shopping brand. Their official sites publish different information.

Who is Min Sihu?

Min Sihu is a founder based in South Korea who founded ActaRoot and OHWUT. His main personal website is msihu.com.

Is Sihu Min another founder?

No. Sihu Min is an alternate English name order for Min Sihu, whose Korean name is 민시후.
