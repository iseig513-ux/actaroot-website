OHWUT — our shopping brand

ActaRoot, Inc. operates OHWUT, a shopping brand serving the U.S. market.

How OHWUT works

OHWUT supports ordinary online shopping and group-driven price drops: a participating product can become less expensive as more customers join.

Product details, availability, orders and purchases are handled on OHWUT's official site, not this corporate information site.
