OHWUT

Online shopping with a different way to reach a lower price.

01

The shopping experience

OHWUT is an online shopping platform for U.S. customers. The service combines ordinary retail purchasing with offers that use participation-linked prices.

OHWUT is the consumer-facing brand behind a connected shopping experience. ActaRoot, Inc. is the company behind it.

02

Participation-based price levels

For eligible offers, the price is connected to participation. As more people join, the offer can reach lower defined price levels.

This connects the experience of purchasing a product with the participation of other shoppers. The conventional shopping experience remains available alongside it.

03

Discovery and sharing

Sharing an eligible offer gives other shoppers the opportunity to participate in the same pricing structure.

The model brings product discovery, purchasing and collective participation together in a single shopping brand.

04

Corporate and founder relationship

Min Sihu founded OHWUT and ActaRoot. ActaRoot, Inc. is the company behind the OHWUT brand.

Use actaroot.com for company information and ohwut.com for OHWUT's shopping service.
