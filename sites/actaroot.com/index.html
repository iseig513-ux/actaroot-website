ActaRoot, Inc.

The company behind OHWUT.

01

Company and commerce

ActaRoot, Inc. is a U.S. corporation founded by Min Sihu. It is the company behind OHWUT, an online shopping platform serving customers in the United States.

ActaRoot is the corporate identity. OHWUT is the consumer-facing shopping brand, combining ordinary purchases with a participation-based approach to product prices.

02

Introducing OHWUT

OHWUT brings online shopping together with eligible offers whose prices can decrease as more shoppers join.

A product offer can have defined participation levels, allowing more customers joining an offer to influence the price. Traditional online shopping remains part of the OHWUT experience.
