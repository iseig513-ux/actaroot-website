ActaRoot, Inc.

ActaRoot, Inc. is a U.S. corporation founded by Min Sihu. ActaRoot operates OHWUT, an online shopping platform built for U.S. customers.

What we build

OHWUT brings everyday online shopping together with a purchase model where the price of participating products decreases as more people join.

The official destination for OHWUT products, shopping and service information is ohwut.com.
