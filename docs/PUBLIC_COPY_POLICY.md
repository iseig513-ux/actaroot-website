# Public editorial policy

Public pages represent ActaRoot, OHWUT and Min Sihu in concise, accurate, professional language. Describe verified identity and the real shopping concept directly. Do not use launch-stage jargon, developer commentary, or statements about a lack of orders, testing, beta status or incomplete implementation in visitor-facing pages and AI-readable public reference files.

- Company identity source: `https://actaroot.com/`.
- Founder identity source: `https://msihu.com/`; alias: `https://min.tienda/`.
- Shopping service source: `https://ohwut.com/`.
- Founder's legal corporate officer registration must not be invented. Personal OHWUT role language reflects the user's stated founder/CEO role.
- Never invent revenue, customer counts, press mentions, awards, investment rounds, employees, or commercial agreements.
- Keep the facts consistent in HTML, JSON-LD and optional companion files.
- Revisit time-sensitive age statements as dates change.

Run `npm run check` before changes are committed. The automatic content validator prevents reintroduction of the specifically prohibited stage/disclaimer vocabulary in public pages.
